# Code of Conduct

EvidenceKit uses the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1, as its behavioral standard.

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, avoid harassment or discrimination, and protect private or security-sensitive information shared during responsible disclosure.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to **a.sarkheil@ezhardynamics.com**. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

The full Contributor Covenant 2.1 text is available from the Contributor Covenant project.
